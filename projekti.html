02Public sectorUNDP

LIMS

Laboratory Information Management System

Selected by UNDP to build the Laboratory Information Management System for the Ministry's National Forensic Center, supporting the country's entire investigative cycle, from crime-scene and ballistic examination through the full chain of custody.

03Public sectorUNDP

SALW Browser

National firearms & ammunition registry

Ministry of Interior of the Republic of Serbia · funded by UNDP

A web application deployed across the Ministry's intranet that gives authorized users fast, centralized access (in line with their access rights) to every database tracking small arms, light weapons and ammunition nationwide.

The system covers reporting as well as lookup, and now holds the records on privately owned firearms in Serbia. UNDP has recognized our team as a responsible and professional partner.

07RecommenderMachine learning

Diet & supplement recommender

Personalized nutrition at consumer scale

A leading global pharmaceutical company

A recommendation engine for a direct-to-consumer supplement platform. It analyzes lifestyle inputs like sleep, mood and daily habits to identify likely nutrient deficiencies, then generates personalized meal plans by mapping thousands of recipes to their nutritional profiles.

With no historical user-feedback data, a collaborative recommender was out of reach, so we built a content-based system with custom similarity scoring, plus a stochastic component to keep suggestions fresh rather than repetitive. The client reported that the project fully met their expectations.
