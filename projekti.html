01

DTech

Pension Fund Management SaaS Platform

  • FinTech
  • SaaS

We built the DTech platform from the ground up — a flexible SaaS product handling fund trading, transaction monitoring and aggregation, investment calculation, pension-fund unit pricing, error handling and reporting for pension funds and unit-linked companies.

At the time of its acquisition by Huddlestock in 2023, DTech served over 6,000 companies, with employees' private pension funds managed through the platform.

02

LIMS

Laboratory Information Management System

National Forensic Center, Ministry of Interior of the Republic of Serbia — funded by UNDP

  • Public sector
  • UNDP

Selected by UNDP to build the Laboratory Information Management System for the Ministry's National Forensic Center, supporting the country's entire investigative cycle — from crime-scene and ballistic examination through the full chain of custody.

The aim is to strengthen the Ministry's capacity across investigations, leading to better-supported convictions. All forensic analysis for the Center is now conducted within the system.

03

SALW Browser

National Firearms & Ammunition Registry

Ministry of Interior of the Republic of Serbia — funded by UNDP

  • Public sector
  • UNDP

Also for the Ministry of Interior and funded by UNDP, we built SALW — a web application deployed across the Ministry's intranet that gives authorized users fast, centralized access to every database tracking small arms, light weapons and ammunition nationwide.

Access is governed by role-based rights, and the system covers reporting as well as lookup. UNDP has recognized our team as a responsible and professional partner, with further collaboration planned.

05

Ofrex

B2B E-Commerce Platform & Data Warehouse

Switzerland

  • Data engineering
  • E-commerce

One of the largest B2B web shops in Switzerland — with 200,000+ products and tens of thousands of business clients — needed both a platform upgrade and real visibility into their data.

We worked with Ofrex's leadership to negotiate the Shopify partnership and design a new system architecture, then led a full migration off their legacy platform and ERP database into a modern data warehouse. Within six months most data had moved to the new platform, which was already generating a return on investment — and giving the team fast, reliable analytics on the state of the business for the first time.

06

Tribute Brand

AI & Computer Vision for Digital Fashion

  • Computer vision
  • Research

We work directly with the team at Tribute Brand — one of the top digital fashion brands in the world — on AI and computer vision research for digital clothing that consumers can buy and "wear" virtually.

One of our projects builds the 3D pose-estimation models that let people try on Tribute's creations in real time using AR. 07

Diet & Supplement Recommender

Personalized nutrition at consumer scale

A leading global pharmaceutical company

  • Recommender
  • Machine learning

We built a recommendation engine for a direct-to-consumer supplement platform — analyzing lifestyle inputs like sleep, mood and daily habits to identify likely nutrient deficiencies, then generating personalized meal plans by mapping thousands of recipes to their nutritional profiles.

With no historical user-feedback data available, a collaborative recommender was out of reach, so we built a content-based system with custom similarity scoring — adding a stochastic component to keep suggestions fresh rather than repetitive. The client reported the project fully met their expectations.
